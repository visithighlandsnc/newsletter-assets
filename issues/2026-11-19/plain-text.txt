*|IF:FNAME|*Hi *|FNAME|*,*|ELSE:|*Hi there,*|END:IF|* if you’re looking for something to do this week, you’ve got plenty of options in Highlands. You can lace up your skates at Kelsey-Hutchinson Founders Park starting today, and on Friday morning families can learn how birds of prey hunt in winter with the Highlands Biological Station. Saturday is a full one: oysters and live bluegrass at Highlander Mountain House in the afternoon, then Mark O’Connor’s Appalachian Christmas at the Highlands Performing Arts Center that night. Take a look at everything happening this week below.


========================================
THIS WEEK IN HIGHLANDS
========================================

RAPID RAPTORS
Highlands Biological Station
Friday, November 20 | 10am-12pm
Nature Center Amphitheater | 930 Horse Cove Rd

Learn how hawks, owls, and other birds of prey adapt to hunting during the colder months. Explore raptor biology and the seasonal changes that affect these remarkable birds. Free and open to all ages, with no registration required. No live raptors will be present.
View Event Details: https://highlandsbiological.org/foundation/programs/#nov-20


BOOK LOVER’S TEA
The Literacy & Learning Center
Friday, November 20 | 1-3pm
First Presbyterian Church, Coleman Hall | 471 Main St

Join award-winning Southern novelist Susan Beckham Zurenda for an afternoon of tea, conversation, and literature. Enjoy traditional finger sandwiches and sweet treats while hearing from the author of Bells for Eli, The Girl from the Red Rose Motel, and her latest novel, No Way Out but Through. Tickets are $50 per person.
Tickets: https://www.theliteracyandlearningcenter.org/events/tea


INTERSTELLAR ECHOES: A TRIBUTE TO PINK FLOYD
Walhalla Performing Arts Center
Friday, November 20 | 7:30-9:30pm
Walhalla Performing Arts Center | 101 N. E. Broad St, Walhalla, SC

Pink Floyd tribute band Interstellar Echoes recreates the group’s sound and stage visuals with close attention to the original recordings. The show pairs familiar favorites with deep cuts, giving longtime fans a chance to hear more of the catalog performed live.
Tickets: https://ci.ovationtix.com/36289/production/1259195?performanceId=11729580


HOMESTEAD SKILLS DROP-IN
Highlands Biological Station
Saturday, November 21 | 10am-12pm
Valentine Meadow | 888 Horse Cove Rd

Try your hand at traditional homesteading skills with hands-on crafts and games led by local education specialists. Discover how everyday tasks were once done by hand and learn about skills passed down through generations. Free and open to all ages, with no registration required.
View Event Details: https://highlandsbiological.org/foundation/programs/#nov-21


OYSTER ROAST & BLUEGRASS
Highlander Mountain House & Ruffed Grouse Tavern
Saturday, November 21 | 3pm
Highlander Mountain House | 270 Main St

Highlander Mountain House is hosting a Lowcountry-style oyster roast featuring fresh Carolina oysters from Pelican Oyster Company, craft beer from Munkie Brewing, and live bluegrass from the Flat Bridge String Band. Enjoy an afternoon on the property with freshly shucked oysters, local brews, and live music.
Tickets: https://resy.com/cities/highlands-nc/venues/highlander-mountain-house-and-the-ruffed-grouse-tavern/events/hmh-oyster-roast-bluegrass-nov-21-st-2026-2026-11-21-6?date=2026-10-05&seats=2


MARK O’CONNOR’S APPALACHIAN CHRISTMAS
Highlands Performing Arts Center
Saturday, November 21 | 7:30pm
Highlands Performing Arts Center | 507 Chestnut St

Three-time Grammy Award winner Mark O’Connor returns by popular demand with An Appalachian Christmas, featuring Maggie O’Connor. The acclaimed violinist brings his signature blend of American folk, bluegrass, and classical music to beloved holiday favorites in a performance that has delighted audiences nationwide.
Tickets: https://prod5.agileticketing.net/websales/pages/info.aspx?evtinfo=587980~854fdbc2-82cf-4081-91dc-a227d39ddd45&epguid=84825558-44fe-4e80-9e6b-820f930d9630&mdy=11/21/2026

Skyline Lodge

Oak Steakhouse Restaurant | 470 Skyline Lodge Rd

DJ Real Bill is taking over Butler’s at Skyline Lodge with classic 90’s records for the next Vinyl & Vibes night. A small bite and complimentary beverage are included, along with games for the evening. Guests are also welcome to bring a favorite record or game of their own.
Tickets: https://www.opentable.com/booking/experiences-availability?rid=1183195&restref=1183195&experienceId=796204
