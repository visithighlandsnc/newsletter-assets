Growing Across North Carolina

New data from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis shows North Carolina’s outdoor recreation economy reached $18.2 billion in 2025, its highest since tracking began in 2012. The industry supported 156,823 jobs statewide, even in the year after Hurricane Helene.
